Language models process massive amounts of text data to uncover patterns in language. This knowledge is stored implicitly in billions of parameters — opaque and hard to inspect.

Knowledge graphs take a different approach. They represent information as entities connected by explicit relationships — making knowledge visible, queryable, and trustworthy.

By combining language models with knowledge graphs, AI systems can retrieve specific facts and reason over structured data with precision and transparency.
